How I Investigate Alerts

Based on hands-on practice in TryHackMe SOC labs.

Getting Started

  1. Review the playbook and note when escalation is needed and when it isn't
  2. Take ownership of alerts from the dashboard, starting with the highest severity

Firewall Alerts

  1. Check the source IP's activity in Splunk
  2. Check the action taken: blocked by the blocklist or allowed
  3. Check whether the destination IP is malicious
  4. Check the URL the employee tried to open

Phishing Email Alerts

  1. Review the email content
  2. Check the legitimacy of the sender
  3. Check the receiver's email activity for past conversations with that sender
  4. Inspect embedded URLs and attachments, including shortened links (e.g., Bitly) that hide the real destination
  5. If malicious, escalate quickly while continuing the investigation
  6. Check whether the attachment was opened or executed
  7. If executed, quarantine the affected user
  8. Search Splunk for everyone who received the same email and who executed it
  9. If more than one user is affected, treat it as a single incident, quarantine them, and alert the affected users

Documenting an Escalation (5Ws)

  • When the alert was triggered
  • What the alert is about
  • Who is affected
  • Where the alert appeared
  • Why it was triggered

Tools: Splunk, URL scanner, TryDetectThis (TryHackMe)
